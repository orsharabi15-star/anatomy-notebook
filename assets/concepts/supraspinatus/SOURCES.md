## Content evidence and deliberate placeholders

- Canonical OIAI: the user's supplied facts, preserved in their supplied order.
- Local course evidence: `lessons/ul-shoulder.html`, lesson 8, pages 53-60. The lesson confirms origin, named nerve and initiation in the first 15 degrees. It does not independently specify the superior facet, C5-C6 or this muscle's arterial supply. These three verification gaps are visible in the page's course note. Original lecture slides were not available in this project mirror.
- Clinical text is limited to the requested standard relationship: the tendon passes under the acromion and is vulnerable to compression and tears.
- The suprascapular nerve and artery have clearly labeled illustration placeholders because individual highlighted, licensed artwork has not yet been selected and verified. Adjacent bony landmarks are visible in contextual images, but do not have individual highlighted figures in this pilot.
- Related deltoid and brachial plexus pages are explicitly future content; no additional concept pages are created. Infraspinatus and suprascapular nerve references link to the actual illustration/section on this same page.
- No GLB was added. The dark placeholder reserves space for a future Supraspinatus model from BodyParts3D via Anatomary. Before adding it, verify the exact model attribution/license, decimate to a few MB, inspect in Blender, and test its browser rendering. CC BY is the requested model-license target, not a claim about a file supplied by this PR.
- Per the user's follow-up, all three flashcard deck names are plain text, with no external flashcard URLs or fabricated deep links. The separate flashcard app is untouched.
